# Week 2 Assignment - Insights

## Overall Summary
The database contains customer, product, order, and order item information. By running different SQL queries, I was able to retrieve, filter, join, and analyze the data to understand sales performance and customer behavior.

## Customer Insights
- The dataset contains 8 customers from different states.
- Customers are spread across cities such as Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Jaipur.
- Rohan Gupta was the highest spending customer with total purchases worth ₹8,397.
- Several customers placed multiple orders, showing repeat purchases.

## Order Insights
- A total of 10 orders were recorded.
- Most orders were successfully delivered.
- Only one order was cancelled and one order was pending.
- Orders with higher values were mostly related to electronics products.

## Revenue Insights
- The total revenue generated from delivered orders was ₹17,191.
- Delivered orders contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Shipped orders also account for a significant amount of revenue and may increase total completed sales once delivered.

## Product Insights
- The database contains 8 products across Electronics, Clothing, and Home categories.
- Electronics products generally have higher prices compared to Home products.
- Running Shoes were the most expensive product, while Cushion Covers were the cheapest.

## Sales Insights
- Wireless Earbuds and Cushion Covers were among the top-selling products based on quantity sold.
- Electronics products appear frequently in customer orders, indicating strong demand.
- Clothing and Electronics categories have average prices above ₹2,000.

## Data Quality Checks
- No duplicate orders were found in the dataset.
- No missing customer names or email values were found.
- No products with invalid prices (zero or negative values) were found.

## Conclusion
The analysis shows that Electronics products generate strong sales, most orders are delivered successfully, and a few customers contribute a large portion of the total revenue.
